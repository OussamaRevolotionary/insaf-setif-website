## 2. Brand Identity

### Logo Assimilation

The logo contains stylized human figures in a circular, upward-moving formation. The visible text is **INSAF** with Arabic text **جمعية إنصاف**. The mark communicates inclusion, movement, children/youth, solidarity, and civic cooperation.

### Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Trust Blue | `#1F9BB4` | Primary identity, links, highlights |
| Institutional Blue | `#2F3E9E` | Headers, official CTAs, trust cues |
| Growth Green | `#20A66A` | Youth development and hope accents |
| Hope Yellow | `#F2C900` | Optimism, children, warmth |
| Solidarity Orange | `#F39A22` | Community action and social warmth |
| Initiative Red | `#E63F4D` | Urgency, advocacy, action moments |
| Ink | `#172033` | Body headings and formal text |

### Typography

- Arabic: **Cairo** or **Tajawal** for clear official Modern Standard Arabic.
- French / English: **Inter** or **Montserrat** for institutional clarity and modern NGO communication.

## 3. Brand Story

### ARABIC
يجسد الشعار أشكالاً بشرية متعددة الألوان تلتقي في حركة دائرية حول مركز واحد، بما يعكس حماية الطفل، طاقة الشباب، وروح التضامن. توازن الألوان بين الثقة والنمو والدفء يجعل هوية إنصاف قريبة من الناس ومفتوحة على الشراكة.

### FRENCH
Le logo présente des figures humaines multicolores réunies dans un mouvement circulaire autour d’un centre commun. Il évoque la protection de l’enfant, l’énergie de la jeunesse et l’esprit de solidarité. L’équilibre entre confiance, croissance et chaleur rend l’identité d’Insaf proche des citoyens et ouverte au partenariat.

### ENGLISH
The logo shows colorful human figures moving in a circle around a shared center. It suggests child protection, youth energy, and the spirit of solidarity. The balance of trust, growth, and warmth makes Insaf’s identity approachable for citizens and open to partnership.
